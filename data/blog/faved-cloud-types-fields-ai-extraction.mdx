A bookmark tells you where something is. It doesn't tell you what you found there: the price of those headphones, how long the recipe takes, what the job pays. Until now you had to open the page again to find out.

**Faved Cloud now turns the pages you save into structured records.** Every page gets a type, each type has its own fields, and Faved can read the page and fill those fields in for you.

## Types and fields

A **type** says what kind of thing a page is, and decides which **fields** it carries. Start from a preset, such as Product, Recipe, Job posting, Real estate listing, Article or Event, or build your own from scratch.

A field can hold text, a number with a unit (€, kg, min), a date, a checkbox, a link, or one or more options from a list you define. Fields can also be **global**, so every record has them whatever its type, which is handy for things like a Project, or Home and Work.

## Extract: let Faved fill in the details

When you save a page, **Extract** reads it and fills in the fields you picked: the price and rating of a product, the ingredients of a recipe, the salary of a job posting. Select fields are matched to the options you already have, and a short hint on a field can steer what it looks for.

Extract can also write a short summary, keep the page's content as clean Markdown, and take a screenshot. Got a backlog? Select any number of records and extract them all at once in the background.

## A view for every type

Each type keeps its own columns and filters. Products line up with their price, brand and rating; recipes with their cuisine and prep time. Filter by any field and sort by any value, such as the cheapest first or the best rated.

## Save from anywhere

Whatever you're doing on the web, saving is a click or a tap away: the [Chrome extension](/docs/cloud/saving/chrome-extension), the [bookmarklet](/docs/cloud/saving/using-browser-bookmarklet) in any browser, the [Apple Shortcut](/docs/cloud/saving/saving-with-apple-shortcut) on iPhone, iPad and Mac, [Android share](/docs/cloud/saving/android-share), or the **Add** button in the app. Every one of them opens the same form, with Extract ready to go.

Already have a library elsewhere? [Import](/docs/cloud/getting-started/importing) your bookmarks from any browser, Raindrop.io or Pocket, and export them any time.
