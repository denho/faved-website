On Android, Faved appears in the Share menu of every app, so you can send a link from your browser, a news reader or a chat straight into Faved's save form.

Android Share comes with the Faved app, so install the app first.

## Install the Faved app

<Screenshot
  src="/static/images/docs/cloud/faved-app-card.webp"
  alt="The Faved App card in Settings › Integrations"
  width={800}
/>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app settings">
    In Faved on your phone, go to **Settings › Integrations › Faved App** and tap **Install app**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Tap **Install** or **Add**. Faved appears on your home screen and in the app drawer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your browser can't install it from there, tap **Show install steps**, or follow [Installing as a PWA app](/docs/cloud/getting-started/installing-as-a-pwa-app#android).

## Share a link

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Share">In any app, open the page or link and tap **Share**.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose Faved">
    Pick **Faved** from the share sheet. Faved opens its save form with the link and title filled
    in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the record type">
    Under **Type**, choose what kind of page this is, such as a product, recipe, or a simple
    bookmark. The form shows that type's fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the fields and save">
    Extract the fields from the page or fill them in manually, then tap **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Some apps share a link inside a piece of text, such as "Look at this: https://…". Faved picks out the link and keeps the rest of the text as the description.

> [!TIP]
> Faved isn't in the share sheet? Make sure it's installed as an app, not just added as a home screen bookmark. Then look under **More** or **Edit** at the end of the share sheet, where Android keeps apps it doesn't show by default.
