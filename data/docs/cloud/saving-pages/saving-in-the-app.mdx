You can add any page to Faved from inside the app, on the web or in the installed app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">Click **Add** in the toolbar.</Step>
  <Step title="Paste a link">
    Paste a link into the URL field. Faved fetches the page's title, description and preview image.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the record type">
    Under **Type**, choose what kind of page this is, such as a product, recipe, or a simple
    bookmark. The form shows that type's fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the fields and save">
    Extract the fields from the page or fill them in manually, then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The form itself, including every field type and the save shortcuts, is covered in [Records](/docs/cloud/guides/records).
