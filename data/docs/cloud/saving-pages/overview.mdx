There are several ways to get a page into Faved. All of them open the same form, where you pick a type, extract fields from the page and save.

The form adapts to the record's type: it shows your global fields plus the fields of the type you pick, and switches to the new type's fields as soon as you choose another one. Values move to fields with the same name and format. The form itself is covered in [Records](/docs/cloud/guides/records).

## In the app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">Click **Add** in the toolbar.</Step>
  <Step title="Paste a link">
    Paste a link into the URL field. Faved fetches the page's title, description and preview image.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the record type">
    Under **Type**, choose what kind of page this is, such as a product or a recipe. The form shows
    that type's fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the fields and save">
    Extract the fields from the page or fill them in, then click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## From your browser or phone

| Method                                                                | Works on                                         |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| [Bookmarklet](/docs/cloud/saving-pages/using-browser-bookmarklet)     | Any desktop or mobile browser                    |
| [Chrome extension](/docs/cloud/saving-pages/chrome-extension)         | Chrome on desktop                                |
| [Apple Shortcut](/docs/cloud/saving-pages/saving-with-apple-shortcut) | iPhone, iPad and Mac, from the Share Sheet       |
| [Android share](/docs/cloud/saving-pages/android-share)               | Any Android app, once the Faved app is installed |

You'll find all of them in **Settings › Integrations**, under **Apps & extensions**. You can also open it from the user menu with **Install apps & extensions**.

## Pages behind a login

The bookmarklet hands Faved the page exactly as you see it, signed in and with its scripts run, so extraction works even on pages Faved couldn't fetch on its own. See [Using browser bookmarklet](/docs/cloud/saving-pages/using-browser-bookmarklet).
