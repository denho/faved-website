## What is Faved Cloud?

Faved Cloud is a bookmark manager that turns web pages into structured records. Every page you save becomes a **record**. Give it a **type**, such as a product, a recipe or a job posting, and the record gets that type's **fields**. Faved can then read the page and fill those fields in for you.

Saving a page, fetching its title and preview image, keeping its content, taking a screenshot and writing a summary are all free. Only the AI step that fills your own fields uses an **AI credit**.

## The ideas behind it

- **Records.** A record is a saved web page: its link, title, description and preview image, plus whatever fields its type adds. See [Records](/docs/cloud/guides/records).
- **Types.** A type decides which fields its records carry. Start from a preset (Product, Real estate listing, Job posting, Article, Recipe, Event) or build your own. See [Types and fields](/docs/cloud/guides/types-and-fields).
- **Fields.** Text, numbers, dates, checkboxes, links and selects, plus three that come from the page itself: a summary, the page content and a screenshot. A field can belong to one type, or be global and appear on every record.
- **Extract.** Faved reads a page and fills in the fields you pick, for one record or up to 500 at once. See [Extracting from pages](/docs/cloud/guides/extracting-from-pages).
- **Options.** A select field holds options, which can be nested, colored and pinned in the sidebar. Your **Tags** field is one of these. See [Options and tags](/docs/cloud/guides/options-and-tags).
- **Views.** Each type keeps its own columns, layout and filters, so a list of products can look like a catalogue and a list of recipes like a cookbook. See [Finding and viewing records](/docs/cloud/guides/finding-and-viewing).

## Your new library

A new account starts with:

- a **Bookmark** type with a **Notes** field. Records saved without a type land here until you make a type of your own; the first type you create becomes the default,
- a **Tags** field in the sidebar,
- a few welcome records that link to these docs,
- a **Get started with Faved** checklist in the sidebar: import your bookmarks, install the app or extension, and save your first link.

## The free trial

Every account starts with a **14-day free trial** of every feature, with **50 AI credits** to try extraction on. No credit card is needed: the trial simply ends, and your records are kept until you pick a plan. See [Plans and AI credits](/docs/cloud/account/plans-and-ai-credits).
