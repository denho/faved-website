## What is Faved Cloud?

Faved Cloud is a bookmark manager that turns web pages into structured records. Every page you save becomes a **record**. Give it a **type**, such as a product, a recipe or a job posting, and the record gets that type's **fields**. Faved can then read the page and fill those fields in for you.

/>

Fields are whatever you want to keep about a page: the price and brand of a product, the address and bedrooms of a listing, the salary range of a job posting, or the cuisine and prep time of a recipe.

A field can hold text, a number with a unit (€, kg, min), a date, a yes/no checkbox, a link, or one or more options from a list you define. You can also save the page's short **summary**, the full **content** as Markdown, and a **screenshot**.

## Key concepts

- **Records.** A record is a saved web page: its link, title, description and preview image, plus whatever fields its type adds.
- **Types.** A type decides which fields its records carry. Start from a preset (Product, Real estate listing, Job posting, Article, Recipe, Event) or build your own. See [Types and fields](/docs/cloud/organizing/types-and-fields).
- **Fields.** A field belongs to one type, or is **global** and sits on every record. Global fields group records of any type together, such as by **Project**, or by Home, Work and Hobby. See [Types and fields](/docs/cloud/organizing/types-and-fields#global-fields-and-type-fields).
- **Extract.** Extraction is Faved's AI at work: it reads the page and fills in the fields you pick, such as the price on a product page, the ingredients of a recipe or the salary in a job posting. A short hint on a field can steer what it looks for. See [Extracting from pages](/docs/cloud/organizing/extracting-from-pages).
- **Views.** Each type keeps its own columns, layout and filters, so a list of products can look like a catalogue and a list of recipes like a cookbook. See [Finding and viewing records](/docs/cloud/organizing/finding-and-viewing).
- **Save from anywhere.** Whatever you're doing on the web, whether reading an article, researching a purchase, browsing listings or scrolling social media, you can save the page to your Faved library in a click or a tap, on desktop or mobile, and get back to what you were doing. See [Saving pages](/docs/cloud/saving-pages/overview).
