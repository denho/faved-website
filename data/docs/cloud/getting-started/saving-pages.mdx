There are several ways to get a page into Faved. All of them open the same form, where you can pick a type, extract fields from the page and save. The form itself is covered in [Records](/docs/cloud/guides/records).

Every capture tool lives in **Settings › Integrations**, under **Apps & extensions**. You can also open it from the user menu with **Install apps & extensions**.

## In the app

Click **Add** in the toolbar, paste a link into the URL field, and Faved fetches the page's title, description and preview image. Fill in or extract the rest, then click **Save**.

## Chrome extension

The [Faved Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/faved-%E2%80%94-save-bookmarks-li/lejmncpaclknlpnpfinmpnfppaokidmi) saves the page you're on in one click, from the toolbar or with a keyboard shortcut. Find it under **Settings › Integrations › Chrome Extension** and click **Add to Chrome**.

## Bookmarklet

The bookmarklet works in any browser, desktop or mobile, without installing an extension. It's a small bookmark that opens Faved's save form for the page you're on.

### Install it on desktop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the bookmarklet settings">
    Go to **Settings › Integrations** and find **Bookmarklet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drag it to your bookmarks bar">
    Drag the **Add to Faved** button onto your browser's bookmarks bar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Install it on mobile, or by hand

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the code">
    Under **Settings › Integrations › Bookmarklet**, click **Code**, then **Copy**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a bookmark">
    Add a new bookmark in your browser and paste the copied code into its URL field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and save">Give it a name such as "Add to Faved" and save the bookmark.</Step>
</Steps>

### Use it

Click **Add to Faved** on any page. A window opens with the page's details, read from the page as you see it. Choose a type, extract the page into its fields or fill them in, then click **Save**.

> [!TIP]
> The bookmarklet hands Faved the page exactly as you see it, signed in and with its scripts run. That means extraction works on pages Faved couldn't fetch on its own, such as pages behind a login. Pages up to 32 MB can be handed over.

If you installed the bookmarklet a while ago, the save window may say **This bookmarklet is an older one**. Click **Get the new bookmarklet** and replace it, so it hands pages over this way.

## Apple Shortcut

On iPhone, iPad and Mac, you can save from the Share Sheet, or from the Services menu on Mac. Go to **Settings › Integrations › Apple Shortcut** and click **Install Shortcut**, then follow [Saving with Apple shortcut](/docs/cloud/getting-started/saving-with-apple-shortcut).

## Android

Install Faved as an app: go to **Settings › Integrations › Faved App** and click **Install app**. If your browser can't install it from there, click **Show install steps**, or see [Installing as a PWA app](/docs/cloud/getting-started/installing-as-a-pwa-app).

Once installed, Faved appears in Android's Share menu, so you can share a link from any app straight into the save form.
