Faved Cloud can be installed as an app (a PWA, or Progressive Web App) on your computer, phone or tablet. It gets its own icon on your home screen or dock, opens in its own window, and on Android adds Faved to the Share menu.

The quickest way is from inside Faved: go to **Settings › Integrations › Faved App** and click **Install app**. If your browser can't install it from there, click **Show install steps** for instructions for your device, or follow the steps below.

## Supported browsers

- **Desktop:** Chrome, Edge and Opera on Windows, Mac and Linux. Safari on macOS Sonoma (14) and later. Firefox on desktop can't install apps; use Faved as a website there.
- **Android:** Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Opera and Samsung Internet.
- **iPhone and iPad:** any browser on iOS and iPadOS 16.4 and later; Safari only on older versions.

## Chrome, Edge and Opera on desktop

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Faved">
Go to [app.faved.to](https://app.faved.to) and sign in.
</Step>
<Step title="Click the install icon">
Click the **Install** icon on the right of the address bar. It looks like a monitor with a download arrow.

> [!TIP]
> No icon? Open the browser menu and choose **Cast, save and share › Install page as app** in Chrome, or **Apps › Install this site as an app** in Edge and Opera.

</Step>
<Step title="Confirm">
Click **Install**. Faved appears in your taskbar, dock, start menu or app launcher.
</Step>
</Steps>

  </Step>

## Android

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Faved in your browser">Go to [app.faved.to](https://app.faved.to).</Step>
  <Step title="Install it">
    Tap the browser's menu and choose **Add to Home Screen** or **Install app**. If an **Install
    app** banner appears at the bottom of the screen, tap that instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">Tap **Add**. Faved appears on your home screen and in the app drawer.</Step>
</Steps>

Once installed, Faved shows up in Android's Share menu: share a link from any app to open it in Faved's save form.
