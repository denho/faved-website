Go to **Settings › Import**. Choose where your bookmarks come from: **From Browser**, **From Raindrop.io** or **From Pocket**. Each one imports a file you export from that service.

<img
  src="/static/images/docs/cloud/import.webp"
  alt="Settings › Import with the From Browser tab, Import as and File folders into"
  width={800}
/>

Whichever you choose:

- **Import as** sets the type every imported record gets. It's your default type unless you pick another.
- **Links already saved are skipped**, so importing the same file twice doesn't create duplicates.
- When the import finishes, Faved shows how many records were imported and how many were already saved.

## From your browser

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export your bookmarks">
    Export your bookmarks as an HTML file from your browser. Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge and most
    other browsers can do this from their bookmarks manager.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the file">
    In **Settings › Import › From Browser**, select the file under **Bookmarks HTML File**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where folders go">
    Under **File folders into**, pick a select field for your folders: an existing one, or **New
    field…**, which creates one called Folders. Choose **None** to import every bookmark without its
    folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">Click **Import from Browser**.</Step>
</Steps>

Your folders become options of the chosen field, nested and ordered the way the browser had them. A link that was in two folders becomes one record with both options. Every imported record also gets an **Imported from browser** option.

## From Raindrop.io

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export from Raindrop.io">
    In Raindrop.io, export your bookmarks as an HTML file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the file">
    In **Settings › Import › From Raindrop.io**, select the file under **Raindrop.io HTML File**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where collections go">
    Under **File folders into**, pick a select field, or **None** to skip collections and tags.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">Click **Import from Raindrop.io**.</Step>
</Steps>

Your collections and tags become options of the chosen field, nested the way your collections were. Every imported record also gets an **Imported from Raindrop.io** option.

## From Pocket

Pocket closed in 2025. If you downloaded your Pocket export before then, you can still import it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the ZIP file">
    In **Settings › Import › From Pocket**, select the ZIP file you exported from Pocket under
    **Pocket ZIP Archive**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">Click **Import from Pocket**.</Step>
</Steps>

Faved brings over your bookmarks, tags, collections and notes:

- **Unread** and **Archived** bookmarks get options under a **Status** parent.
- **Collections** become options under a **Collections** parent, with their descriptions kept.
- Notes on collection bookmarks go into the **Notes** field.
- Every imported record gets an **Imported from Pocket** option.

The options go into your library's **Tags** field.

> [!TIP]
> After importing, select the new records and use [bulk actions](/docs/cloud/guides/bulk-actions) to change their type or extract details from their pages.
