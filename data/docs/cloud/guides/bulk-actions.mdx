Select records with their checkboxes. A bar appears showing how many are selected; click the count to select or clear the whole page. Then choose an action.

## Change type

Gives the selected records a new type. They get that type's fields. Values with nowhere to go are kept, hidden, and come back if you switch them back. See [Records](/docs/cloud/guides/records#changing-a-records-type).

## Edit field

  width={634}
/>

Sets or clears one field on every selected record. Choose a field, then:

- enter a value and click **Set**, or
- click **Clear** to empty it.

Only fields that all the selected records share are offered. When records of several types are selected, that means their global fields.

For a select field, each option has three states: ticked puts it on every record, a dash leaves it as it is, and unticked takes it off. Tick **Replace** to keep only the ticked options. Then click **Apply changes**.

## Extract

Runs Extract on the selected records in the background. See [Extracting many records at once](/docs/cloud/guides/extracting-from-pages#extracting-many-records-at-once).

## Delete

Deletes the selected records after you confirm.

> [!WARNING]
> Deleting removes the records, their field values and their extracted pages. This can't be undone.
