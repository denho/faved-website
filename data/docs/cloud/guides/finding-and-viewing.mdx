## Search

Type in **Search everywhere…** above the list. Faved matches as you type, ignoring case, across titles, links, descriptions and field values. For page content, only the beginning of the page is searched. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to clear it.

## Types

The **Types** list at the top of the sidebar narrows the list to one type, or shows **All types**. Each type brings its own columns, layout and filters with it.

## Filters

Each field can be filtered:

- **Text and links:** is, is not, contains, does not contain, starts with, does not start with, ends with or does not end with. Links can also be filtered by **domain is**.
- **Numbers and dates:** **At least** and **At most**.
- **Select fields and other formats:** pick from the values records hold.
- **Any field:** **Has a value** or **Is empty**.

Select fields filter from the sidebar; everything else filters from chips above the list. Move any field between the two with **Move to toolbar** or **Move to sidebar**. Drag the chips to reorder them; when there are many, the rest fold behind **Show all**.

## Sorting

Open **Sort by** and choose a field. Double-click to flip the order. New records come first by default.

## Layouts and fields

Click **View** to choose a layout and the fields it shows:

- **List view**, **Cards view** or **Table view**.
- **Fields:** turn each field on or off, and drag them into order. **Show built-in only** hides your custom fields.
- **Field names:** show the field's name beside its value, in cards and lists.
- **Sync field settings between layouts:** keep the same fields across all three layouts.
- **Reset to the default field order**.

In the table, drag a column's edge to resize it.

## A view for each type

Your layout, fields, column widths and filters are saved for each type, on your account. So a list of products can be a table with Price and Brand, while recipes are cards with an image and a cooking time. Choosing **All types** shows the default view, which types you haven't arranged yet also use.

## Paging and links

Choose how many records to show per page: 10, 25, 50 or 100. Your search, type, filters, sort and page are kept in the page's address, so a link to it opens the same view.

## Reading and screenshots

Long values such as page content and summaries show a short preview in the list. Click it to open the full text in a reader. Click a screenshot to see it full size.
