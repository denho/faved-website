You can export your records at any time, even after your trial or subscription ends, as a standard bookmarks HTML file. Browsers, other bookmark managers and Faved itself can all import it.

<Screenshot
  src="/static/images/docs/cloud/export.webp"
  alt="Settings › Export with Type and Folders from"
  width={800}
/>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Export">Go to **Settings › Export**.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose the records">Under **Type**, choose **All types** or a single type.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose the folders">
    A bookmarks file has one folder tree, so one select field becomes the folders. Under **Folders
    from**, pick that field, or **None** to put every record at the top level.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Click **Export records**. Faved downloads a file named like `faved-bookmarks-2026-10-01.html`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's in the file

- Every record you chose, once, with its title and link.
- The chosen field's options as folders, nested as they are. A record with several options goes in the first one.
- Records with none of the options at the top level. Empty folders are left out.

> [!NOTE]
> Descriptions, notes, dates and other fields aren't exported. The file holds what a bookmarks file can: titles, links and folders.
