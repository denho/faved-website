A record is a saved web page. It has a link, a title, a description and a preview image, plus the fields of its type. This page walks through the record form, which you'll see whenever you add or edit a record, whether from the app, the bookmarklet, the extension or a share menu.

## Adding a record

Click **Add** in the toolbar. You can also save from your browser or phone; see [Saving pages](/docs/cloud/saving-pages/overview).

The form has these parts, from top to bottom:

1. **URL.** Paste a link. With **Auto-fetch** on (it is by default), Faved fetches the page's title, description and preview image as soon as the link changes. Use the refetch button to fetch them again. If the link has no `https://`, Faved adds it.
2. **Preview card.** Shows the title, description and image. Click it to edit any of them, then click **Done editing**.
3. **Type.** Choose the record's type. "The type decides which fields this record carries." **Manage types…** at the bottom of the list opens [Types and fields](/docs/cloud/guides/types-and-fields).
4. **Extract from the page.** Pick which fields Faved should fill from the page, and extract now or when you save. See [Extracting from pages](/docs/cloud/guides/extracting-from-pages).
5. **Fields.** Global fields come first, then the type's own fields, in the order set in Types and Fields. A globe icon marks a field that's on every record.
6. **+ New field.** Adds a field right from the form, either to this record's type or to all types.

Then click **Save**, or **Save & extract** when extraction will run on save. **Save & add another** keeps the form open for the next one.

> [!TIP]
> Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>↵</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> on Windows and Linux) to save.

### Filling in fields

- **Numbers** step with the arrow keys.
- **Dates** have a **Today** button, or **Now** when the field includes a time.
- **Links** have buttons to copy and open them.
- **Checkboxes** are on or off.
- **Summary** and **Page content** are Markdown. Switch between **Write** and **Preview** in the field's header. Long values open in a reader with **Read in full**.
- **Screenshot** shows the captured page. **Remove** deletes it.

A value that doesn't fit its field's format, such as letters in a number field, is marked, and the record can't be saved until it's fixed.

## Editing a record

Click a record in the list, or choose **Edit record** from its menu. Change anything and click **Save changes**. **Save as copy** saves your edits as a new record instead.

### Stepping through records

When you open a record from the list, the header shows its position, such as **3 of 48**. Use the up and down arrows next to it, or <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>↑</kbd> / <kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd>, to move to the previous or next record without closing the editor. The order follows the list as it's currently filtered and sorted. If you have unsaved changes, Faved asks before moving on.

## Changing a record's type

Pick a different type in the form. Values move to fields with the same name and format in the new type. If the new type has no field for some of your values, Faved asks before switching. On a saved record, those values are kept, hidden, and come back if you switch back.

To change the type of many records at once, use [bulk actions](/docs/cloud/guides/bulk-actions).

## Duplicates

While you add a record, Faved checks whether you've saved the page before. A badge in the form's header shows the result: **No duplicates**, a number of duplicates found, or a number of records from the same site. Click it to see them, and open any match to compare or edit it.

When matching links, Faved ignores `www.` and similar prefixes, tracking parameters such as `utm_source`, the order of other parameters, and a trailing slash.

## Deleting records

Delete a record from its menu, from the trash button in the form, or with <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⌫</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Backspace</kbd>). To delete several, select them and use [bulk actions](/docs/cloud/guides/bulk-actions).

> [!WARNING]
> Deleting removes the record, its field values and its extracted pages. This can't be undone.

## The record menu

Each record in the list has a menu with **Edit record**, **Open link**, **Copy link**, **Extract…**, **Make a copy** and **Delete**.
