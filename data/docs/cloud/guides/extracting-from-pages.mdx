**Extract** reads a web page and fills in the fields you pick.

## What each field gets

- **Your own fields** (Text, Number, Date, Checkbox, Link, Select) are filled by the AI from what the page says.
- **Summary** gets a short summary written by the AI.
- **Page content** gets the page's text as clean Markdown, without menus, footers or cookie banners.
- **Screenshot** gets a snapshot of the page.
- **Title**, **Description** and **Preview image** are refreshed from the page itself.

Only fields with **Filled by extraction from the page** turned on can be extracted. See [Types and fields](/docs/cloud/guides/types-and-fields#field-settings).

## Extracting while you save

The record form has an **Extract from the page** row. Expand it to see the fields that will be filled, and click them to pick or skip each one. Your choice is remembered.

- Click **Extract** to read the page now. On a new record, the values land in the form before anything is saved, so you can check them first.
- Or leave **Extract on save** on. It's on by default for new records, and Faved reads the page in the background after you save.

The row shows what it will cost, such as **Uses 1 AI credit** or **No AI credits**, and **History** shows past extractions for the record.

## Extracting from the list

<Screenshot
  src="/static/images/docs/cloud/extract-popover.webp"
  alt="The Extract popover for a record, with the fields to fill and the AI credit it uses"
  width={384}
/>

Choose **Extract…** from a record's menu. Pick the fields to fill, then click **Extract from page**. Picking only Title, Description and Preview image refreshes them from the page for free.

To extract many records at once, select them in the list and click **Extract** in the bulk bar. Before you start, the popover shows how many pages will be read and how many AI credits that uses. The run carries on in the background, and you can follow it in [Activity](#activity).

## Activity

**Activity**, at the bottom of the sidebar, lists every extraction: what it asked for, what it cost, and anything that went wrong. Filter by **All**, **Succeeded** or **Problems**. A bulk run appears as one group with its pages inside. While a run is going, click **Cancel** to stop it.

## What it costs

Filling your own fields with AI uses one AI credit per page; everything else Extract does is free. See [What uses an AI credit](/docs/cloud/account/plans-and-ai-credits#what-uses-an-ai-credit).
