## Settings

Open **Settings** from the gear in the sidebar or from the user menu. It has five tabs:

- **Account:** your name, email and password. If you signed up with Google, you can also set a password here.
- **Integrations:** the Chrome extension, bookmarklet, Apple Shortcut, Android share and the app. See [Saving pages](/docs/cloud/getting-started/saving-pages).
- **Import** and **Export:** see [Importing bookmarks](/docs/cloud/guides/importing) and [Exporting records](/docs/cloud/guides/exporting).
- **Billing:** your plan, your AI credits and payment.

The user menu also has **Theme**, to choose Light, Dark or Match system.

## Subscribing

During your trial, **Billing** shows how many days are left. Pick **Monthly** or **Yearly**, choose a plan and pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or a local method in your country. PayPal and bank transfer are available on request: [email us](mailto:hello@faved.to). See [Plans and AI credits](/docs/cloud/account/plans-and-ai-credits) for what each plan includes.

## Managing your subscription

Under **Settings › Billing**, click **Manage billing** to open the payment provider's customer portal, where you change plan or cancel. Upgrades and downgrades work the same way, and your new allowance shows in Faved within a minute of the change.

On Basic, **Upgrade to Plus** moves you to Plus.

## Cancelling and refunds

You can cancel at any time, with no minimum term and no fee. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for, and we keep your records afterwards for whenever you come back.

Refunds are available within **14 days** of payment on monthly plans, and within **30 days** on yearly and lifetime plans. [Email us](mailto:hello@faved.to) and we'll sort it out.

## When a trial or subscription ends

When your trial ends without a subscription, or a subscription lapses, Faved shows **Keep your library working**. Your records are safe. Pick a plan to carry on where you left off.

Until then, you can still **export your records** from the account menu on that page. Your records are kept until you're back.
