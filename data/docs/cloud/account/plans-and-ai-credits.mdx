## The free trial

Every account starts with a **14-day free trial** of every feature. It includes **50 AI credits** for the whole trial. No credit card is needed: when the trial ends, nothing is charged and your records are kept until you pick a plan.

## Plans

| **Basic**    | $5 a month  | $48 a year ($4 a month)   | 100 a month |

## What uses an AI credit

**One AI credit is one page put through the AI to fill your own fields**, however many fields come back. Extracting from the same page again uses another credit. It's the same whether Faved fetched the page or the bookmarklet handed it over.

These are **free** and don't use credits:

- fetching a page's title, description and preview image,
- keeping the page's content,
- taking a screenshot,
- generating a summary.

Your credit balance and this month's use are shown under **Settings › Billing** in **AI extraction**. Before you extract, the Extract row and popover show how many credits a run will use.

## Running out

When your credits run out, AI extraction pauses until they renew at the start of your next month; on a trial, until you subscribe. Everything else keeps working, including the free parts of extraction.

Need more sooner? On a paid plan, buy a top-up from **Settings › Billing** with **Add credits**:

- **100 AI credits for $5**
- **500 AI credits for $20**

Top-up credits never expire, and are used once the month's allowance is spent. They aren't available during the trial. Or move to Plus for 500 credits a month.

## Fair use

Records, fields and types have no plan limit. Fair use applies, because Faved is for your own library, not for bulk scraping:

- **Library:** up to 100,000 records.
- **Page reads:** 500 over a trial, 5,000 a month on a paid plan. A page read is any page Faved fetches for you, the free ones included. It's separate from AI credits.

Normal use never comes close. If an account does, we email first.
