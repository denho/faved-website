## The free trial

Every account starts with a **14-day free trial** of every feature. It includes **50 AI credits** for the whole trial. No credit card is needed: when the trial ends, nothing is charged and your records are kept until you pick a plan.

## Plans

| Plan         | Monthly      | Yearly                      | AI credits  |
| ------------ | ------------ | --------------------------- | ----------- |
| **Basic**    | \$5 a month  | \$48 a year (\$4 a month)   | 100 a month |
| **Plus**     | \$20 a month | \$192 a year (\$16 a month) | 500 a month |
| **Lifetime** | \$120 once   | —                           | 100 a month |

Every plan includes unlimited records, fields and types, the web archive (page content and screenshots), every way to save pages, import and export, bulk actions and daily backups. Lifetime is Basic, paid once, for as long as Faved Cloud exists, and is offered for a limited time.

For teams, [email us](mailto:hello@faved.to?subject=Faved%20for%20Teams%20request) and we'll arrange a setup.

## What uses an AI credit

**One AI credit covers one page the AI reads to fill your own fields**, whether it fills one field or twenty. Extracting from the same page again uses another credit. A page Faved fetched and a page the Chrome extension or bookmarklet handed over cost the same.

These are **free** and don't use credits:

- fetching a page's title, description and preview image,
- keeping the page's content,
- taking a screenshot,
- generating a summary.

Your credit balance and this month's use are shown under **Settings › Billing** in **AI extraction**.

## Running out

When your credits run out, AI extraction pauses until they renew at the start of your next month; on a trial, until you subscribe. Everything else keeps working, including the free parts of extraction.

Need more sooner? On a paid plan, buy a top-up from **Settings › Billing** with **Add credits**:

- **100 AI credits for \$5**
- **500 AI credits for \$20**

Top-up credits never expire, and are used once the month's allowance is spent. They aren't available during the trial.

## Fair use

Records, fields and types have no plan limit. Fair use applies, because Faved is for your own library, not for bulk scraping:

- **Library:** up to 100,000 records.
- **Page reads:** 500 over the whole trial, and 5,000 a month on every paid plan, Lifetime included. Every time Faved reads one of your pages counts, whether it fetched the page or the Chrome extension or bookmarklet handed it over, and whether or not it fills any fields. Page reads are counted separately from AI credits.

Normal use never comes close. If an account reaches a limit, Faved stops reading pages or adding records and asks you to [email us](mailto:hello@faved.to), and we'll sort it out.
