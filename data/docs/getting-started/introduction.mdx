## What is Faved?

Self-hosted Faved is the classic, open-source Faved bookmark manager that helps you organize, categorize, and access your saved web content from anywhere, on any device.

There are two ways to use it:

- **Self-host it for free.** Faved is open-source and runs on your own computer, server, or cloud infrastructure, keeping your data entirely under your control — no third party collecting your habits, no risk of your bookmarks vanishing if a company shuts down.
- **Or use Faved Cloud.** [Faved Cloud](https://app.faved.to/signup) adds types, fields and AI extraction, with hosting, backups, updates and email support included. Start with a 14-day free trial, then plans from $5 a month. See the [Faved Cloud docs](/docs/cloud/getting-started/introduction).

Faved Cloud has grown into its own edition, with types, fields and AI extraction, while self-hosted Faved remains the classic bookmark manager. You can bring a Cloud library to self-hosted Faved at any time: export it from Cloud as a bookmarks file and [import it](/docs/guides/importing-bookmarks#import-from-your-browser) here. Export from self-hosted Faved is planned.

## Key Features

- **Advanced Tagging System**: Go beyond simple folders with a powerful nested tagging system. Customize tags with colors, pin frequently used tags to the top, and create a personalized organizational structure that works for you.

- **Smart Bookmark Management**: Automatic fetching of titles, descriptions, and preview images, plus built-in duplicate detection to keep your collection clean.

- **Instant Search & Sorting**: Find bookmarks as you type, with flexible sorting across collections of any size.

- **Bulk Actions**: Delete, retag, and refetch metadata across many bookmarks at once.

- **Works on Any Device**: Fully responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile, and installable as a PWA for a near-native mobile experience—no separate apps required.

- **Light & Dark Mode**: Automatically syncs with your system theme for comfortable viewing in any lighting condition.

- **Integrations**: Save the current page in one click with the <a href={siteMetadata.chromeExtensionUrl} target="_blank" rel="noopener">Chrome extension</a>, save from any browser with a lightweight bookmarklet, or send links via the native Share menu on iPhone, Mac, and iPad.

- **Import & Migration**: Import from Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge with folder structure preserved, and migrate from Raindrop.io, Pocket, and other tools retaining collections and tags.

- **Open Source**: Fully transparent codebase that you can inspect, customize, and extend.


## Who is Faved For?

Faved is the perfect solution for:


- **Researchers & Power Users**: Organize extensive libraries of links to your sources with advanced tagging and categorization that scales to thousands of bookmarks.

- **Developers & IT Professionals**: Keep links to documentation, tutorials, code snippets, and technical resources organized with a system built by developers, for developers.

- **Privacy Advocates & Self-Hosters**: Run Faved on your own infrastructure to keep your bookmarks completely private and immune to service shutdowns, lock-in, or sudden price increase. The open-source, self-hosted version gives
you permanent ownership and control of your link collection.

- **Teams & Organizations**: Faved for teams is available on Faved Cloud only. [Get in touch](mailto:hello@faved.to?subject=Faved%20for%20Teams%20request) and we'll help set it up.
